# The four cases (Kasus)

> 🇩🇪 Deutsch: [`../../de/grammatik/kasus.md`](../../de/grammatik/kasus.md)

German marks a noun's role in the sentence with case. This mostly changes the article
(and sometimes the noun itself).

## Nominative (Nominativ) — the subject
Who or what performs the action. Also after `sein`, `werden`, `bleiben`.
- **Der Mann** liest. — The man reads.

## Accusative (Akkusativ) — the direct object
Who or what the action directly affects. Also after: durch, für, gegen, ohne, um.
- Ich sehe **den Mann**. — I see the man.
- (only masculine changes: der → den)

## Dative (Dativ) — the indirect object
To whom something is given / said. Also after: aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu;
and after gefallen, helfen, danken, gehören.
- Ich gebe **dem Mann** das Buch. — I give the book to the man.

## Genitive (Genitiv) — possession
Whose. Also after: wegen, während, trotz, (an)statt.
- Das Auto **des Mannes** — the man's car.

## Article table

| | masculine | feminine | neuter | plural |
|-----|------|-----|------|--------|
| Nom | der  | die | das  | die |
| Akk | den  | die | das  | die |
| Dat | dem  | der | dem  | den (+n on the noun) |
| Gen | des (+s) | der | des (+s) | der |

## Common mistakes
- Forgetting that `mit`, `nach`, `bei`, `von`, `zu` always take the dative.
- Two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen: in, an, auf, unter, über, vor, hinter,
  neben, zwischen): accusative for motion *into* a place, dative for a location.
